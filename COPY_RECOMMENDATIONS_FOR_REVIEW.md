# Visible Copy Recommendations for Review

**Status:** Review only. No recommendation in this document has been applied to the public-facing website.

## Purpose

The website now has metadata-only SEO, answer-engine optimization, and generative-engine optimization improvements. The next opportunity is to make important customer-facing facts easier to understand and verify on each page. These suggestions are written to strengthen clarity, conversion, and citation readiness without overstating capabilities or repeating keywords.

| Page | Recommended visible-copy improvement | Why it helps | Approval needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Add a brief, plain-language definition of Workshop as a nationwide print brokerage and sourcing partner near the first service introduction. | Gives visitors and answer engines a concise answer to “What does Workshop Creative Group do?” | Yes |
| Home | Add a qualification to the savings statement, such as “Savings vary by specifications, quantities, timing, and freight.” | Keeps the “up to 20%” promise clear and supportable. | Yes |
| Home | Add an outcome-oriented line to the two-step invoice process: “We compare production, materials, freight, and turnaround for a like-for-like quote.” | Explains how a comparable quote is evaluated. | Yes |
| About | Add a short “How we work” section that explains supplier matching, job specification review, proofing/quality oversight, and delivery coordination. | Strengthens trust and clarifies the brokerage model. | Yes |
| Large Format Printing | Add a scannable list of common applications already supported by the page—such as event displays, retail graphics, banners, window graphics, wall graphics, and wayfinding—with a short use case for each. | Helps buyers match their need to the page and gives search systems clearer topical entities. | Yes |
| Graphic Design | Add a compact list of deliverables, such as print-ready files, campaign pieces, sales collateral, signage artwork, and brand assets. | Reduces ambiguity about what “graphic design” includes. | Yes |
| Print Procurement | Add a transparent comparison statement: Workshop reviews the existing job specifications and may suggest equivalent production options where appropriate. | Sets accurate expectations and avoids implying every job will receive identical pricing or specifications. | Yes |
| Request a Quote | Add an expectation-setting line describing what a helpful invoice or project brief includes—quantity, size, material, due date, delivery location, and production notes. | Improves submission quality and reduces back-and-forth. | Yes |

| Gallery | Add a brief introduction explaining that individual projects show a mix of printing, graphic design, branding, and procurement work. | Provides context before users browse visual examples. | Yes |
| Gallery | Review client/project labels for consistent wording and add concise captions to priority portfolio items. | Supports accessibility, clearer context, and answer-engine image understanding. | Yes |
| Blog | When the blog is intentionally reintroduced into navigation, use practical article titles that answer one business-print question at a time. | Supports long-tail research and citation-ready content without adding thin articles. | Yes |

## Recommended Review Standards

Every approved visible change should retain the company’s actual service scope, use the “up to 20%” statement only where it is supported by the comparison context, and avoid invented response times, certifications, customer claims, pricing, testimonials, or guarantees. New FAQs should answer only questions that the company can confidently and consistently support.

## Suggested Approval Method

Please mark each row **Approved**, **Revise**, or **Do Not Use**. Once approved, changes can be applied page by page and kept separate from the completed metadata update.
